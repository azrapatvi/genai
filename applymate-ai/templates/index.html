How to get a Gmail App Password

  1. Open your Google Account: myaccount.google.com/security and turn on 2-Step Verification (App Passwords only work with it enabled).
  2. Go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.
  3. Type any name, for example ApplyMate, and click Create.
  4. Google shows a 16-character password. Copy it and paste it in the field above (spaces are fine).
  5. Use this app password, not your normal Gmail password.

If you can't see the App Passwords page, 2-Step Verification is probably off, or your account is managed by a school/work admin who has disabled it. Your password is kept in memory only while the app is running and is used only to send your emails.
